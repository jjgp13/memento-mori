    WHAT MATTERS MOST

    1. Not smokingStrongUp to about 10 years. Quitting at any age helps.
    2. Moving more, staying fitStrongCardio fitness is one of the strongest single predictors of survival.
    3. Close relationshipsStrongLoneliness carries risk on the scale of smoking.
    4. Diet qualityStrongMore plants and whole foods, less processed meat.
    5. Healthy weight and blood pressureStrongBoth drive heart disease, the top cause of death.
    6. Drinking littleModerateHeavy drinking clearly shortens life. Benefits of light drinking are doubtful.
    7. Sleeping 7–8 hoursModerateShort sleep is linked to higher risk.
    8. Walking after mealsEmergingLowers blood-sugar spikes. No direct lifespan study yet.

    Effects use broad risk ratios from population studies. They show direction and rough size, not a promise.
